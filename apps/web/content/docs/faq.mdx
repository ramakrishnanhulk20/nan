## Is there interest or a fee?

No. You repay exactly what you received, and Nan charges nothing on top. The only cost is the network fee for each call: the first real borrow paid about 1.3 US cents and the repay about 0.4 cents, on 25 November 2026.

## What happens if I never repay?

Once the due time plus one day of grace has passed, anyone can mark the advance missed. The advance stays open, and a wallet with an open advance cannot borrow here, so that wallet never borrows again unless it pays. Nothing is taken from the wallet. More on [If you do not repay](/docs/if-you-do-not-repay).

## Can someone else repay for me?

Yes. `repay(borrower)` works from any wallet and settles that borrower's advance, which counts for the borrower. Anything sent above the amount owed goes back to the payer in the same transaction.

## Why does the site not ask me to connect a wallet?

Your Agent ID lives in a Binance Agentic Wallet, and your own agent sends the call from that wallet. The site only reads the chain and writes the slip you paste, so it never needs a signature, a QR scan or a seed phrase.

## Why do I need an Agent ID?

Lending with nothing locked up only works if a borrower who skips a payment cannot come back as someone new. A wallet is free to make, but the registry gives a wallet one Agent ID for good, which can never move or be destroyed. The contract checks the registry in the same transaction that pays you.

## What can the owner do with my wallet?

Nothing. The owner cannot take BNB from your wallet, edit your step or history, change an advance you already have, or stop you repaying. What the owner can do is to the pool: add or withdraw its BNB, pause new advances, change four settings inside fixed bounds after a 12 hour public delay, issue and cancel passes, recover a token sent by mistake, and hand over ownership in two steps. The full list is on [Owner powers](/docs/owner-powers).

## Why was my borrow refused?

The contract stops at the first rule that fails:

- Paused: the owner has paused new advances. Repaying still works.
- Advance open: this wallet already has an advance. Repay it first.
- Too early: after an on-time repayment the next advance waits for the last term to end; after a late one, 7 days.
- No Agent ID: the registry does not show a name for this wallet that points back to it, or shows a different name from the one it first borrowed with.
- Pace: every first advance released so far is taken. The home page says when the next opens, or a [pass](/docs/passes) skips the wait.
- Pool empty: the pool cannot cover the advance right now. Above the first step it also keeps 0.03 BNB back for newcomers. Nothing is wrong with your name.

## Does it work without Binance AI Pro?

Yes. Any agent that uses the Binance Agentic Wallet can borrow and repay through the [Nan skill](/docs/agent-skill), which sends the same calls with `baw` after one preview and one yes.

## Is there a token?

No. Nan has no token, and the pool lends and takes back BNB only. A token sent to the pool by mistake can only be sent on to the owner, with `rescueToken`.
