HOW TO READ THE RECORD
Each attack carries the error its rule says it must be refused with. REFUSED means it was refused with
exactly that error. HOLDS is a check on the books. SERVED is a control: a call that must succeed, so
that the refusals next to it mean something. Anything else would start with two exclamation marks.
